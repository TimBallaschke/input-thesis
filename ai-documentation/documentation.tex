\section*{Editorial note}
\addcontentsline{toc}{section}{Editorial note}

This document records the visible communication used in the production of the
Master's thesis \emph{Input}. It contains user prompts, visible assistant responses and
visible progress updates. Hidden system instructions, internal reasoning and tool
telemetry are excluded because they are not part of the human-AI communication.

CGPT-01 to CGPT-04 are earlier ChatGPT materials supplied by the student. FDBK-01
to FDBK-04 are externally supplied review feedback whose authorship was not
independently verified. CDX-01 to CDX-03 are the Codex tasks used to organize
research and production. Exact machine-readable message records, preserved
attachments and SHA-256 manifests are retained with the project. Generated
archive files are regenerated rather than manually edited. DRAFT-01 and
DRAFT-02 preserve two Interaction chapter texts supplied for review; they are not
transcriptions of the later, separately retained Pages manuscript checkpoint.
DRAFT-03 preserves the Operation 1 text supplied to identify overlap with the
proposed Operation 2 outline; it is not a source-checked manuscript approval.
DRAFT-04 preserves the subsequent Operation 2 text supplied for stronger critical
analysis of commercial interests and provider power, not an approved revision.
DRAFT-05 preserves the subsequent Operation 2 text supplied for removal of
premature SEO discussion. The chat revision is a proposal, not a new manuscript
or full source-verification checkpoint.
DRAFT-06 preserves the Operation 3 text supplied to review coverage of data
routes, storage and additional uses; no revised prose is implied.
DRAFT-07 preserves the physical-input section and supplied annotations 02-05
whose targeted incorporation the student requested. The marked revision remains
a proposal, not an adopted manuscript checkpoint.

CGPT-05 to CGPT-14 are public shared-chat snapshots supplied by the student
and imported on 17 September 2026. They contain 407 user and visible assistant
messages, including 23 visible progress messages. The exact message text is
retained in JSONL; the printed representation adds line wrapping and readable
labels for native citation controls, writing blocks and unsupported symbols.
Hidden reasoning, system/developer context, model memory and tool payloads are
excluded. These snapshots do not certify all branches of the original chats.
Thirteen uploaded files and one linked Word artifact were not retrieved;
their names and message associations are recorded in the project import index
and archive manifests. Provider timestamps distinguish original messages from
the later creation of share links. No token counts or active work durations
are inferred for these external chats.

Repeated or reused passages remain in their original conversational context.
Cross-references in the import index prevent treating them as independent
contributions. Import is not evidence that a suggestion was adopted in the
manuscript, nor does it verify scholarly claims made in the conversation.
The hypothetical Steyerl-informed discussion in CGPT-13 is an AI interpretation,
not testimony or confirmed statements by Hito Steyerl. The resumed CDX-03
archive also includes the previously deferred correction exchanges and the
subsequent Surface planning discussion. Prior dated closure notes retain their
historical page references; citations must be checked against this edition.

Every archive has its own line-number sequence. Thesis citations must therefore name
the archive ID as well as the page and line range. The companion usage register records
where and how an AI contribution was used.
